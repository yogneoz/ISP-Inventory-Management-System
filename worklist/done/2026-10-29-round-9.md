## Goal

The warranty directory derived one 12-month countdown from an issue or
acquisition date and called the result both a customer warranty and a vendor
warranty. Neither promise was captured at the document that created it, no
product term existed, and the same constant drove both sides. This round
replaced that with two explicit coverage objects — what a supplier owes us and
what we give a customer — where the **exact expiry date is authoritative**, the
term snapshot and start basis are kept beside it, and a correction supersedes
rather than overwrites history.
