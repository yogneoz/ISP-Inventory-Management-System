## Goal

Rounds 1–4 finished the login payload, and the 2026-10-27 watch-list
measurement answered the remaining uncapped arrays as payload items. One
trigger stayed open — `inventory_stock` — but two things the payload work left
behind were worth doing first: the stock screens looked rows up with
`stock.find(...)` inside product × branch loops in 8 screens (30 call sites),
quadratic in the one array that can still grow and re-run in the render body on
every keystroke in Branch Stock, and EXCH-1 had left a dead client limb behind
when the Device Exchange picker moved to the paged device read. Round 5 was
deliberately small: remove the cost that is already visible, pin the dead path
closed, then decide the payload question with the post-fix numbers instead of a
hunch.

## Measured

STOCK-1, jsdom probe on a 2,000 products × 20 branches fixture (40,000 stock
rows — the watch-list model; the demo DB holds 18 products / 3 branches /
32 rows, so this is a scale fixture, not production data), one warm run each:

| Derivation | Before | After | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pure product totals (`products × branches × stock.find`) | **35,748 ms** | **47.9 ms** | ≈ 746× |
| Physical Audit consolidated matrix | **35,458 ms** | **52.4 ms** | ≈ 677× |
| Branch Stock mount (real component) | **34,604 ms** | **338–406 ms** | ≈ 85–102× |
| Branch Stock re-render (one toggle click) | **31,449 ms** | **121–171 ms** | ≈ 184–259× |

Totals are identical before and after (`same totals: true`); the residual
338–406 ms mount is jsdom rendering 20 rows × 20 branch cells, not the lookup.

STOCK-2 re-measured the global stock read through the real
`BOOTSTRAP_TABLES.stock` mapping on the local demo DB: **163.7 B raw / 15.4 B
gzip per row** against the recorded 143 B / 27 B, so the honest band is
**≈ 143–164 B raw and ≈ 15–27 B gzip per row**. A global ALL-scope read at
2,000 × 20 is **0.59–1.03 MiB gzip** ($10,000 rows → 150–264 KiB; $100,000 rows
→ 1.47–2.57 MiB), i.e. it straddles the ≈ 1 MiB trigger instead of clearly
passing it.
