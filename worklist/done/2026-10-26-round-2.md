## Goal

Take the three reads that grow with customers, branches and years off the login
payload or out of the hot refresh, without folding in the parked structural
work (A5, A4b, B3, B4, C4). Regional context for the sizes: the dataset is
expected to grow past 12k customers and cover Nepal-wide operations, so every
measurement was taken at a modelled 1× and 10×.
