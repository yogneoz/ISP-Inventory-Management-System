## Goal

Round 2 removed what the client *receives*, but the full bootstrap still paid
for ledgers it never sent: at national volume (12k customers at 1×, 120k at
10×, Nepal-wide) the remaining cost was linear in the customer base and the
fiscal year's operations. This round finished the login path: the summary
aggregates in SQL, and `shipments`, `customers` and `customerDevices` leave the
payload for paged, server-scoped reads.
