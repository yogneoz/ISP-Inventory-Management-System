## Goal

Rounds 2–3 narrowed what a login receives and finished its read path. Two
recorded gaps were left, and both were about reach rather than size alone:
`fixed_assets` still shipped whole — 29 aliased columns, no cap and no
fiscal-year filter, one row per capitalized unit for the life of the business,
handed by App to 19 screens — and the Device Exchange picker still filtered the
bounded legacy `GET /api/customer-devices` array (2,000 newest rows) in the
browser, so an older deployed unit could not be exchanged at all. Round 4 took
the last table that grows with the installed base off the payload and moved the
last legacy-array consumer onto the paged device read.
